# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Disagree with ideas, not people.

Not accepted: harassment, insults, personal attacks, discriminatory language,
and publishing other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break
these rules, and may block repeat offenders. Report problems privately to the
maintainers through https://github.com/OWNER/silk/security/advisories/new or by
contacting a maintainer directly.
